[Les Misérables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Misérables_(musical)) was the most impactful live performance I have ever experienced: and I saw it about forty-five years ago, so that is saying something (not sure what, but certainly _something_).

Recently I missed one of my morning pills and went back to take it, so the tweaked line "One Pill More" came to mind.

<!--more-->

With five voiced parts, here are revised lyrics to [One Day More](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_More) possibly appropriate to anyone having to comply with a difficult drug regimen.  Ends with Éponine's part as that seemed appropriate.

```
[A] One pill more
[A] Another pill, another effect on me
[A] This never-ending road to recovery
[A] The doctors who seem to know my life
[A] Will surely diagnose me another time

[A] One pill more
[B] I would have died before today
[B] How can I live with out this treatment?

[A] One pill more
[B&C] Tomorrow I’ll [you’ll] be here to stay
[B&C] And yet each day, my [your] world has started

[D]  One more pill in the early morn
[E]  Will they ever go away

[D] One more pill to end my day with
[E] They will always be with me

[D] What a life I have right now
[E] And I swear I will be true

[D] But at least I am alive
```
