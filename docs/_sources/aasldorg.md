AASLD is the leading organization of scientists and health care professionals committed to preventing and curing liver disease.  <!--more-->  We foster research that leads to improved treatment options for millions of liver disease patients. We advance the science and practice of hepatology through educational conferences, training programs, professional publications, and partnerships with government agencies and sister societies.
